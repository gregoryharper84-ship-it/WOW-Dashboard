# V17 Team/Event Sport Parity Contract

All cataloged team/event sports traverse the same orchestration stages:

`DISCOVERY -> CANONICAL IDENTITY -> SPORTING EVIDENCE/HYDRATION -> EXACT SPORT MODEL -> CALIBRATION -> GOVERNANCE -> V17_TERMINAL_REDUCER`

Equal treatment does **not** mean identical models or invented capability. Sport-specific fitted models, evidence sources, settlement rules, and calibration artifacts remain authoritative. If the exact fitted specialist is absent, the row remains visible as `MODEL_UNAVAILABLE`. If the specialist exists but required evidence/calibration is missing, it is `MODEL_INPUTS_INSUFFICIENT`. Provider/auth/quota failures retain their own acquisition status and must not be relabeled as model absence.

The catalog is MLB, NFL, NBA, WNBA, NCAAF, NCAAB, NHL, SOCCER, TENNIS, PGA, MMA, and BOXING. Every cataloged sport must have an explicit parity-health row whether or not it is currently model-capable.

Sportsbook prices, implied probability, recent hit rates, generic Elo, public projections, and narrative judgment may not substitute for the exact sport model. Market evidence remains downstream of sporting probability.

Publication is row-scoped. A healthy/registered sport route is permission to attempt governed scoring, not a blanket `probability_publishable=true`. Final row publication and rank eligibility remain controlled by the sport package, calibration/audit/final-refresh chain, and `V17_TERMINAL_REDUCER`.

`can_execute=false` is invariant.
