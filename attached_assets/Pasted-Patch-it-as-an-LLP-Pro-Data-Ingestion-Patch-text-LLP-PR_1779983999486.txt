Patch it as an **LLP Pro Data Ingestion Patch**.

```text
LLP PRO DATA INGESTION PATCH

Purpose:
LLP must use a pro data stack for team-side markets before approving ML, spread, totals, F5, or derivative bets.

Primary data sources:
1. OpticOdds or OddsJam
   - opening odds
   - current odds
   - consensus odds
   - line movement
   - closing odds
   - sportsbook timestamps
   - CLV tracking

2. SportsDataIO
   - starters
   - lineups
   - injuries
   - depth charts
   - team/player stats
   - projected/confirmed status

3. Official backup sources
   - MLB.com probable pitchers
   - official league/team injury reports
   - weather/park sources where applicable

Engine-calculated fields:
- no-vig implied probability
- model win probability
- edge %
- Kelly %
- CLV delta
- CLV grade
- market timing score
- final decision

Required tables:
- odds_snapshots
- team_context
- lineup_status
- injury_status
- model_outputs
- clv_log
- bet_decisions

LLP workflow:
1. Pull odds snapshots from OpticOdds/OddsJam.
2. Store opening/current/consensus/closing lines.
3. Pull starters, lineups, injuries, and context from SportsDataIO.
4. Confirm official starters/lineups where available.
5. Calculate no-vig implied probability.
6. Run LLP model win probability.
7. Calculate edge %.
8. Calculate Kelly %.
9. Grade CLV opportunity.
10. Run Layer 4 Team Bet Synthesis.
11. Output BET / SMALL BET / LEAN / WATCH / PASS.

Hard rule:
Do not approve LLP team bets from stale lines, likely winners, or market discrepancies alone. A stale/soft line creates a Candidate only. Approval requires official context, matchup validation, edge %, Kelly %, and acceptable failure paths.

Failure tags:
- missing-odds-feed
- missing-lineup-status
- missing-starter-confirmation
- stale-market-not-actionable
- clv-without-validation
- fake-market-edge
- candidate-promoted-too-early
```

Fast legacy platform/Claude command:

```text
Patch LLP Team Betting Engine with LLP PRO DATA INGESTION PATCH.

Add OpticOdds/OddsJam as the odds + CLV source, SportsDataIO as the starter/lineup/injury source, and official league sources as backup confirmation. Store odds snapshots, team context, lineup status, injury status, model outputs, CLV logs, and bet decisions.

Make win probability, no-vig probability, edge %, Kelly %, CLV grade, timing score, and final decision engine-calculated fields. Do not approve team bets from stale lines or likely winners alone. Stale/soft lines are Candidate only until Layer 4 validation clears.
```
