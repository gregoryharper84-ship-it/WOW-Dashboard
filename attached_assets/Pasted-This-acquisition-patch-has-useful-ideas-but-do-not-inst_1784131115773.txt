This acquisition patch has useful ideas, but do not install it as written. It predates the active July 15 LLP governance patch and contains several risks.

### What is good

Keep these features:

* Provider-specific market aliases (`moneyline → h2h`)
* PrizePicks decimal-multiplier adapter
* UTC event normalization
* Structured provider diagnostics
* Full acquisition escalation before `DATA_UNOBTAINABLE`
* Multi-book no-vig reconstruction
* Explicit contract-stage reporting
* Six-label enforcement
* Replay tests for the failed July 14 boards

### What must be corrected

1. **Do not use broad aliases like `"Washington"` or `"Indiana"`.**
   They are ambiguous across leagues. Identity must be league-scoped using canonical team IDs.

2. **Do not silently reverse home and away.**
   Reversed order can help locate an event, but the engine must preserve venue and selected-side identity. Otherwise it could price the wrong team.

3. **A fixed ±90-minute tolerance is unsafe.**
   Use league/date/team identity first, then a configurable tolerance. Doubleheaders require provider event IDs or game numbers.

4. **Official league sources provide identity/status, not sportsbook prices.**
   They cannot satisfy the market/no-vig gate.

5. **Proxy, manual, screenshot, or generic web prices require source ceilings.**
   They cannot promote an entry as though they were direct sportsbook quotes.

6. **The proposed projection has market leakage.**
   This is the biggest problem:

```python
build_projection(market_baseline=consensus_no_vig_probability)
```

If the model starts from consensus and later claims an edge against that same consensus, the edge is partially circular. Build the independent projection first. Use market consensus only for comparison, reconciliation, and optional shrinkage that is explicitly disclosed.

7. **Reconstructed consensus needs coherent books.**
   Require two-sided quotes from at least two independent books, aligned market identity, acceptable freshness, and outlier filtering.

8. **It must inherit the July 15 handshake, PostgreSQL ledger, actual-price EV gate, market firewall, calibration ceiling, and structure rules.**

9. **Do not activate a July 14 patch beside the newer July 15 patch without precedence.**
   Rename it as an extension of the active patch.
