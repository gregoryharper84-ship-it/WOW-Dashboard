According to a document from **2026-06-27**, patched and active for future LLP runs in this chat. The six final LLP labels remain locked, `LLP_PLAYABLE` remains small-stake only, and scaling still requires ledger/CLV maturity, exposure clearance, and final-lock discipline.   

```text
LLP STAKE-CONFIDENCE PATCH — v16.1A — ACTIVE

Purpose:
Add stake_tier and confidence_tier to every LLP candidate without changing the six-label final taxonomy.

New required output fields:
final_label:
confidence_tier: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH
stake_tier: PASS / SMALL / MEDIUM / HIGH
recommended_stake:
max_allowed_stake:
stake_cap_reason:
confidence_reason:
big_stake_status: ELIGIBLE / BLOCKED
big_stake_blockers:

Label-to-stake ceiling:
LLP_CUT      -> PASS
LLP_REJECT   -> PASS
LLP_SCOUT    -> PASS
LLP_WATCH    -> PASS
LLP_PLAYABLE -> SMALL only
LLP_APPROVED -> SMALL / MEDIUM / HIGH eligible

Core rule:
confidence_tier cannot override final_label.
stake_tier cannot outrank final_label.
A HIGH-confidence pick can still be SMALL stake if final label, CLV, bankroll, exposure, or final-lock rules cap it.

SMALL stake:
Allowed for clean LLP_PLAYABLE or capped LLP_APPROVED.
Range: 0.10u–0.25u, subject to bankroll and session cap.

MEDIUM stake:
Allowed only for LLP_APPROVED.
Requires:
- verified no-vig edge
- model probability 58%+
- edge clears tier threshold
- timestamp current
- final-lock recheck complete
- no contradiction
- no exposure breach
Range: 0.25u–0.50u.

HIGH stake:
Allowed only for elite LLP_APPROVED.
Requires:
- model probability 60%+ or independently validated severe price edge
- edge 5%+
- final-lock recheck complete
- no steam against side
- no injury/starter/lineup/weather contradiction
- no exposure cap conflict
- ledger maturity supports scaling
- CLV bucket supports graduation
Range: 0.50u–1.00u max.

Hard blockers for MEDIUM/HIGH:
- final_label is not LLP_APPROVED
- no no-vig edge
- no timestamp
- final lock skipped
- market moved against side
- missing or incomplete ledger
- fewer than 25 logged candidates
- CLV rate below required graduation level
- bankroll/session cap conflict
- same-game or same-script exposure breach
```

The exposure caps remain unchanged: max 3 LLP bets/day, 1.5u normal daily cap, 2.0u hard cap only after calibration maturity, 1.0u max per game, and 1.25u same-script cap.
