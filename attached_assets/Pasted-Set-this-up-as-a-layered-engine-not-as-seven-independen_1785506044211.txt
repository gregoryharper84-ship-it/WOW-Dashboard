Your existing WOW v16 skills already provide much of the middle and final structure. Your slip optimizer already requires verification, role-aware ledgers, bidirectional scoring, weakest-leg elimination, fragility review, and shrinking the card when no replacement qualifies. wow_slip_probability_optimizer_skill_v2.md

Floor/ceiling classification

A practical initial classifier:

FLOOR_DRIVEN
25th percentile clears the line
BALANCED
median clears the line but 25th percentile does not
CEILING_DEPENDENT
only the 65th percentile or higher clears the line
VOLATILE
distribution width or role uncertainty exceeds threshold

For a MORE selection:

if p25 > line:
    FLOOR_DRIVEN
elif median > line:
    BALANCED
elif p65 > line:
    CEILING_DEPENDENT
else:
    LOW_PROBABILITY

The reverse applies to LESS.

Module C — Scenario Survival Engine

Files:

wow_engine/
  scenarios/
    wnba_scenarios.py
    scenario_runner.py
    correlation_graph.py

This replaces a single deterministic projection with scenario-weighted probability.

Required scenarios:

NORMAL_CLOSE_GAME
SLOW_PACE
FAST_PACE
BLOWOUT_WIN
BLOWOUT_LOSS
REDUCED_MINUTES
FOUL_TROUBLE
LOW_SHOOTING_EFFICIENCY
HIGH_SHOOTING_EFFICIENCY
ROLE_EXPANSION
ROLE_COMPRESSION

Do not weight all scenarios equally. Assign probabilities using game-specific evidence.

Example:

{
  "NORMAL_CLOSE_GAME": 0.42,
  "SLOW_PACE": 0.13,
  "FAST_PACE": 0.11,
  "BLOWOUT_WIN": 0.10,
  "BLOWOUT_LOSS": 0.07,
  "REDUCED_MINUTES": 0.05,
  "FOUL_TROUBLE": 0.04,
  "ROLE_EXPANSION": 0.04,
  "ROLE_COMPRESSION": 0.04
}

Each scenario produces its own prop probability. The final probability is:

P(prop) = Σ P(scenario) × P(prop | scenario)

Required outputs:

{
  "scenario_survival_rate": 0.61,
  "weighted_hit_probability": 0.57,
  "worst_material_scenario": "SLOW_PACE",
  "largest_failure_path": "REDUCED_SHOT_VOLUME",
  "scenario_probability_floor": 0.49
}

Do not use a simplistic rule that the prop must win in 75% of named scenarios regardless of their likelihood. Use the weighted survival probability and separately report how many material scenarios it survives.

Suggested initial gate:

weighted scenario probability lower bound >= 65%
no scenario with weight >= 15% may have hit probability below 40%

3. Add the Cross-Slip Exposure Governor

Files:

wow_engine/
  portfolio/
    cross_slip_exposure.py
    thesis_registry.py
    session_ledger.py

This is different from the existing weakest-leg optimizer.

The weakest-leg optimizer evaluates one slip. The cross-slip governor evaluates the entire session.

Your existing optimizer already requires removing or replacing the weakest leg and shrinking the slip when no better replacement exists. WOW-v16-MLB-Pitcher-Failure-Path-Patch-Pack.md The new governor must prevent the same player or thesis from appearing repeatedly across several cards.

Required session-level keys:

player
event
market family
stat component
game-script thesis
team dependency
role dependency

Example:

{
  "player": "Kayla McBride",
  "event_id": "MIN-TOR-2026-07-30",
  "thesis_id": "MCBRIDE_OFFENSIVE_VOLUME_OVER",
  "slips_used": 3,
  "total_entry_exposure": 125,
  "duplicate_markets": [
    "PRA_MORE_19.5",
    "PRA_MORE_22.5"
  ]
}

Hard rules

Start with:

maximum_player_appearances_per_session = 1
maximum_event_appearances_per_session = 2
maximum_identical_thesis_appearances = 1
maximum_alternate_lines_same_distribution = 1

Do not allow a 19.5 PRA and 22.5 PRA on the same player to count as diversification. They are the same underlying distribution.

A possible exception:

maximum_player_appearances = 2
only when:
  calibrated_lower_bound >= 75%
  opportunities are not component/composite duplicates
  cross-slip exposure governor explicitly passes

I would keep that exception disabled during initial forward testing.

I recommend four formal patches.

PATCH-WNBA-001 — Opportunity Stability Gate

No WNBA prop may receive YES_MODEL_QUALIFIED unless:
opportunity_stability_status=PASS
role_confidence meets market-specific threshold
minutes distribution is present
primary teammate dependencies are resolved

Failure labels:

WNBA_REJECT_UNSTABLE_OPPORTUNITY
WNBA_HOLD_ROLE_UNCERTAIN
WNBA_REJECT_ROTATION_VOLATILITY

PATCH-WNBA-002 — Component and Ceiling Risk

PRA and combined props require component distributions and covariance.
Made-shot props require attempt and efficiency distributions.
Ceiling-dependent MORE props cannot receive HIGH confidence.

Failure labels:

WNBA_REJECT_COMPONENT_MODEL_MISSING
WNBA_HOLD_CEILING_DEPENDENT
WNBA_REJECT_ATTEMPT_VOLUME_UNSUPPORTED

PATCH-WNBA-003 — Scenario Survival Gate

Every WNBA prop requires scenario-weighted probability.
A material adverse scenario cannot be omitted.
Final probability must be scenario-weighted, not baseline-only.

Failure labels:

WNBA_REJECT_SCENARIO_MODEL_MISSING
WNBA_HOLD_SCENARIO_FRAGILE
WNBA_REJECT_GAME_SCRIPT_DEPENDENT

PATCH-PORTFOLIO-001 — Cross-Slip Exposure Governor

One player/thesis per session by default.
Alternate lines on the same distribution are duplicate exposure.
No final slip may be presented until session exposure passes.

Failure labels:

REJECT_DUPLICATE_PLAYER_EXPOSURE
REJECT_DUPLICATE_THESIS
REJECT_CROSS_SLIP_CONCENTRATION

9. Regression tests

Before activating, require these tests:

1. A high projected PRA with low opportunity stability is rejected.
2. A made-2s prop with insufficient attempt volume cannot pass from recent makes alone.
3. A ceiling-dependent over cannot receive HIGH confidence.
4. A prop that passes the normal scenario but fails weighted adverse scenarios is held or rejected.
5. The same player on two slips triggers cross-slip rejection.
6. Two alternate PRA lines for the same player are treated as duplicate exposure.
7. A role change forces a new role-split ledger.
8. Missing teammate status blocks role-dependent scoring.
9. A failed original MORE does not automatically approve LESS.
10. No verified replacement causes the slip to shrink.
11. Every settled prop writes opportunity and scenario predictions to the ledger.
12. can_execute=false is always preserved.

Best implementation order

Build it in this order:

Stage 1
Cross-slip exposure governor
Opportunity stability engine
Stage 2
Shot-volume and component distributions
Floor/ceiling classifier
Stage 3
Scenario survival engine
Role redistribution model
Stage 4
Calibration ledger
Threshold tuning
Custom GPT orchestration skill

The first two additions would immediately address the most visible failures: unstable WNBA opportunity and repeated exposure to the same player or thesis. The scenario and covariance models should follow once the underlying role and volume data are reliably captured.
