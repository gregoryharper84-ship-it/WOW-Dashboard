The best setup is not one app. For your WOW final-lock needs, the best tool is a custom “PrizePicks Final Lock” Replit/Google Sheets dashboard fed by a few data sources.

Best stack for your exact need:

Live PrizePicks payout + current PP line	PrizePicks app/manual final check or OpticOdds PrizePicks feed	PrizePicks itself shows picks as live and says multipliers are subject to change, so final lock must verify the app immediately before entry.   OpticOdds advertises PrizePicks real-time odds/props, line movement alerts, consensus lines, and 100+ book coverage.

For the math layer:

Power slip probability = p1 × p2 × p3 ...
Break-even slip probability = 1 / payout_multiplier
Required independent per-leg probability = (1 / payout_multiplier) ^ (1 / number_of_legs)
Slip EV per $1 = slip_probability × payout_multiplier - 1

For Flex slips, you need a separate payout table and should calculate all hit combinations, not just full-sweep probability.

Important: I would not rely on direct PrizePicks scraping unless it is through a compliant licensed feed. PrizePicks terms prohibit using robots/spiders/automatic means to access, monitor, or copy site/app material.  

Bottom line:
Use OpticOdds if you want the closest paid all-in-one feed. Use The Odds API + Props.Cash + WNBA/RotoWire + a Replit EV dashboard if you want the best practical WOW setup. The missing piece is not research anymore — it is a machine-enforced final-lock EV gate.
