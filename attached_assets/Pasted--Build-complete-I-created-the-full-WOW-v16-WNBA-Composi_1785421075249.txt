## What is now patched

### WNBA composite lane

The new expert:

* Models points, rebounds and assists jointly rather than independently.
* Compares points, rebounds, assists, P+R, P+A, R+A and PRA.
* Does not default automatically to PRA.
* Uses role-matched ledgers, minutes distributions, teammate status and outlier isolation.
* Classifies composites as `THREE_PATH_STABLE`, `TWO_PATH_STABLE`, `ONE_PATH_DEPENDENT`, `ROLE_FRAGILE`, or `LINE_TOO_EFFICIENT`.
* Separates promotional lines from standard lines.
* Requires 20 unique graded player-games before the forward-test ceiling can be reviewed.
* Counts multiple thresholds from one player-game as one calibration observation.
* Does not count a DNP or void as a model hit.

### MLB regression firewall

The MLB v2 skill now enforces:

```text
MLB_K_LESS=WATCH_ONLY
MLB_OUTS_MORE=MODEL_QUALIFIED_HOLD ceiling
```

Every strikeout LESS must show how much of its probability comes from:

```text
low strikeout rate
opponent contact
short outing
workload restriction
market support
```

When more than 50% of the LESS case depends on an early exit:

```text
HIGH confidence prohibited
```

Pitching-outs MORE must publish the unconditional probability of reaching the exact out threshold, including early hooks, pitch caps, bullpen pressure and manager behavior.

### Cross-ticket governance

The new governor treats all cards from one slate as a single exposure portfolio. It blocks:

* Exact legs repeated across Flex and Power cards.
* Alternate thresholds treated as independent picks.
* Repeated pitcher-direction theses.
* Related points/PRA, rebounds/PRA or assists/PRA exposure.
* Power cards copied from an overlapping Flex card.
* Forced replacement legs added merely to maintain card size.

Repeated legs remain separate in the financial ledger but count once in model calibration.
