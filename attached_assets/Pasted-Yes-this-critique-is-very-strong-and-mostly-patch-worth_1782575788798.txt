Yes — this critique is very strong and mostly patch-worthy. I would accept the core ideas, but I would modify the wording so the engine does not become too blunt and kill valid PrizePicks stale-line value.

The most important correction: sharp-market disagreement must be directional. A PrizePicks line that is stale versus a sharp sportsbook is not automatically bad. It is bad only when your chosen side is on the wrong side of sharp movement.

My verdict

Recommendation	Verdict	Notes
Sharp vs retail market weighting	Adopt, but directional	Very needed. PP/DFS should never be treated as confirmation. It is the target market.
Settlement loopback/calibration ingestion	Adopt fully	This is one of the biggest missing operational pieces.
House rules matrix	Adopt fully	Especially important for DNP, partial play, injury returners, and platform-specific grading.

1. Sharp vs retail weighting is correct, but needs a side-aware rule

The proposed REJECT_FALLING_KNIFE is useful, but I would not make it blanket.

Example:

Scenario	Correct action
PP line is higher than sharp book and you want MORE	Reject or downgrade. You are buying a bad number.
PP line is higher than sharp book and you want LESS	Potentially good. This may be exactly the stale value you want.
PP line is lower than sharp book and you want MORE	Potentially good.
PP line is lower than sharp book and you want LESS	Reject or downgrade.

So the patch should say:

Reject only when the selected side is opposed to sharp market direction or when sharp movement has erased the edge.

Better labels:

SHARP_ANCHOR_CONFIRMED
SHARP_ANCHOR_CONFLICT
REJECT_SHARP_CONFLICT
REJECT_FALLING_KNIFE
MARKET_VERIFIED_HOLD
FLIP_CANDIDATE

For your current WNBA props, this matters a lot. Rhyne Howard rebounds MORE 3.5 with an 8/10 L10 ledger is not enough by itself. If sharp books sit 3.5/4.5 and lean over, it can survive. If sharp books are 3.5 juiced under or already moved down, it becomes a hold/reject.

2. Settlement loopback is a must-have

This is the best recommendation in the whole critique.

Your WOW model has calibration rules, but calibration cannot work unless every settled result gets fed back in a structured format.

Minimum settled ledger fields:

date
sport
player/team
market
side
book/platform
submitted_line
closing_line
submitted_odds_or_payout
closing_odds_or_projection
model_probability
market_probability
edge
result
CLV
failure_tag
dominant_failure_tag
slip_type
pick_count
payout_multiplier
slip_EV
actual_slip_result

Hard rule I would add:

If SETTLED_LEDGER is missing, stale, or not updated within 18 hours, no FINAL_APPROVED labels are allowed. Maximum label becomes MODEL_QUALIFIED_HOLD or MARKET_VERIFIED_HOLD.

That prevents the model from pretending it is calibrated when it is not.

3. House rules matrix is absolutely needed

This should be patched immediately.

For PrizePicks, sportsbook props, DFS pick’em, and fantasy score markets, settlement rules are not interchangeable. A player who starts and exits early can be graded very differently depending on the platform.

Add this to the failure path layer:

HOUSE_RULES_MATRIX_REQUIRED = true
Required fields:
platform
market_type
DNP_rule
partial_play_rule
stat_correction_rule
combo_stat_rule
fantasy_score_rule
void_push_rule
discount_goblin_demon_rule
protected_play_rule_if_any

Then add:

REJECT_HOUSE_RULES_VULNERABILITY

This should fire when:

player has elevated injury/return risk
AND platform grades partial play as action
AND usable probability depends on normal minutes/role

For example, a hamstring-return player’s MORE prop may look good by L10, but if 6 minutes of play still grades as a loss, the usable probability needs a haircut or a full reject.

4. Latency and liquidity gate is good, but should be adapted for PrizePicks

For sportsbooks, liquidity is real: limits, market depth, timing, and price availability matter.

For PrizePicks, the better version is:

EXECUTION_FRICTION_GATE

Required final-lock checks:

current PrizePicks line
current payout multiplier
current slip type
current pick count
line age under 15–30 seconds
injury/status checked
market/projection comp checked
no payout downgrade
no line movement against side
no duplicate exposure
no unresolved source conflict

Possible labels:

REJECT_EXECUTION_STALE
REJECT_PAYOUT_CHANGED
REJECT_LOW_LIMIT_UTILITY
REJECT_LINE_MOVED_AGAINST_SIDE

I would use low liquidity more for sportsbook/LLP. For PrizePicks, the bigger issue is line/payout drift and slip EV decay, not traditional liquidity.

5. Same-game correlation should not be handled natively by the LLM

To answer the question at the end:

Right now, I would not trust the engine to calculate same-game joint probabilities natively inside the prompt. That should be handled by an external matrix or Replit module.

The LLM can classify correlation:

DIRECT_OVERLAP
SAME_PLAYER_COMPONENT_OVERLAP
TEAMMATE_USAGE_NEGATIVE
TEAMMATE_USAGE_POSITIVE
PACE_ENVIRONMENT_POSITIVE
BLOWOUT_SHARED_RISK
INJURY_ROLE_DEPENDENCY
LOW_CORRELATION

But the actual math should be external:

independent_slip_probability = p1 × p2 × p3
correlation_adjusted_probability = external model output
slip_EV = adjusted_probability × payout - 1

For now, without a validated correlation model, your safest rule should be:

No same-player overlapping stat props in the same slip.
No same-team props in the same slip unless correlation is explicitly classified and EV-adjusted.
No Power Play approval when correlation is unknown.
Flex slips require correlation-adjusted hit-combination math, not independent-leg math.

Example direct block:

Rhyne Howard MORE rebounds
+
Rhyne Howard LESS rebounds + assists
=
DIRECT_COMPONENT_CONFLICT
Auto reject same slip.

Example caution block:

Rhyne Howard rebounds MORE
+
Allisha Gray assists MORE
=
same-team environment dependency
needs pace/rotation/game-script review
not auto-approved

Patch I would actually add

Use this as the clean patch register:

WOW-PATCH-2026-06-27-SHARP-MARKET-SETTLEMENT-HOUSE-RULES-CORRELATION-GATE
Purpose:
Prevent false approval caused by retail consensus, stale calibration, platform settlement traps, execution friction, and unpriced same-game correlation.
1. Sharp Market Anchor
- PrizePicks/DFS lines are target markets, not confirmation markets.
- Market confirmation must anchor to sharp/no-vig sportsbook lines when available.
- Retail books may supplement but cannot override sharp disagreement.
- Discrepancy must be evaluated directionally by selected side.
- If selected side is opposed to sharp movement or sharp fair line, label REJECT_SHARP_CONFLICT or REJECT_FALLING_KNIFE.
- If stale PP line is favorable in the same direction as sharp market, keep as MARKET_VERIFIED_HOLD pending final lock.
2. Settlement Loopback
- Every run must ingest SETTLED_LEDGER from prior 24–72 hours.
- Required: result, CLV, closing line, submitted line, model probability, slip EV, failure_tag.
- If missing or stale >18 hours, downgrade calibration health to MONITOR.
- MONITOR blocks FINAL_APPROVED unless manual override reason is logged.
3. House Rules Matrix
- Every prop/slip must identify target platform settlement rules.
- DNP, partial play, stat correction, combo stat, fantasy score, push/void, and protected play rules must be known.
- If partial-play risk is elevated and platform grades partial play as action, apply injury_haircut.
- If haircut erases edge, label REJECT_HOUSE_RULES_VULNERABILITY.
4. Execution Friction / Liquidity
- Final lock must verify current line, payout, slip type, pick count, player status, market comp, and timestamp.
- Low-liquidity or fast-moving markets require live-check age under 15 seconds.
- Any payout change, line move against side, or stale timestamp blocks approval.
- Labels: REJECT_EXECUTION_STALE, REJECT_PAYOUT_CHANGED, REJECT_LOW_LIQUIDITY, REJECT_LINE_MOVED_AGAINST_SIDE.
5. Correlation EV Gate
- LLM may classify correlation but may not invent exact joint probability.
- External Replit/matrix module should calculate adjusted slip probability.
- Direct overlap, same-player component overlap, and unresolved same-game correlation block Power Play approval.
- Flex slip EV must use hit-combination math, not independent full-sweep math only.

My bottom line: this is a strong patch set and should be integrated, but I would rewrite REJECT_FALLING_KNIFE so it only fires when the chosen side is fighting the sharp market. Otherwise, you could accidentally reject the exact stale PrizePicks value you are trying to find.
