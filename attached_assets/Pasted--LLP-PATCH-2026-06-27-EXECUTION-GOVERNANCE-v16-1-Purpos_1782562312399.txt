## LLP-PATCH-2026-06-27-EXECUTION-GOVERNANCE-v16.1

**Purpose:** Prevent model signals from becoming bets without verified price edge, timing validity, contradiction clearance, calibration, and exposure control.

### 1. Final LLP Labels

Only these final labels are allowed:

* `LLP_APPROVED`
* `LLP_PLAYABLE`
* `LLP_WATCH`
* `LLP_SCOUT`
* `LLP_REJECT`
* `LLP_CUT`

Banned as final action labels:

* `LEAN`
* `CONDITIONAL`
* `FLIP_CANDIDATE`
* `SOURCE_CONFLICT`
* `DATA_UNOBTAINABLE`
* `NO_BET`
* `STALE_LINE`

Those may only appear as status tags or failure tags, never as final betting labels.

### 2. Action Rules

`LLP_APPROVED` = bettable only if current line/odds still match approval snapshot.
`LLP_PLAYABLE` = small-stake playable only.
`LLP_WATCH` = no bet yet.
`LLP_SCOUT` = track only.
`LLP_REJECT` = no edge, wrong side, or bad price.
`LLP_CUT` = invalid slate, stale market, missing price, missing timestamp, source conflict, or unavailable market.

### 3. Price Edge Required

No verified price edge = no bet.

Required fields before `LLP_PLAYABLE` or `LLP_APPROVED`:

* book
* odds
* line
* side
* market
* timestamp
* opener if available
* consensus or no-vig fair probability
* model probability
* edge
* source
* final-lock recheck

Formula:

`edge = model_probability - no_vig_market_probability`

Minimum edge thresholds:

* Liquid main markets: `≥ 1.5%`
* WNBA / lower-liquidity markets: `≥ 2.0%`
* Derivatives, F5, 1H, team totals: `≥ 2.5%`
* Alt / niche markets: `≥ 3.0%`
* No no-vig comp: max `LLP_SCOUT`

### 4. Probability Caps

* `<52%` = `LLP_REJECT` unless opposite side has value
* `52–54%` = max `LLP_WATCH`
* `55–57%` = max `LLP_PLAYABLE`
* `58–60%` = `LLP_APPROVED` eligible only with clean market edge
* `>60%` = rare; requires independent validation and no contradiction

Probability alone never approves a bet.

### 5. Timing / Price Decay

Approval expires if any of these occur:

* ML moves `10+ cents` against the side
* spread/total/team total moves `0.5+`
* starter, lineup, injury, or weather changes
* opener/consensus changes the thesis
* timestamp becomes stale
* market disappears

Freshness windows:

* More than 6h before start: line checked within 30m
* 2–6h before start: within 15m
* Less than 2h before start: within 5m
* Less than 30m before start: final lock within 2m

No final lock = max `LLP_WATCH`.

### 6. Steam Protocol

If consensus implied probability moves `1.5%+` against the candidate, rerun.

If consensus implied probability moves `2.5%+` against the candidate, downgrade to `LLP_WATCH` unless edge still clears.

Do not chase steam if edge is compressed below the required threshold.

A better price than the market after steam requires stale-line suspicion review.

### 7. Contradiction Hard Kills

Hard kill conditions:

* market move against thesis
* key player/starter/news contradiction
* lineup contradiction
* stale price
* missing timestamp
* source conflict
* weather/park contradiction
* wrong slate
* unavailable price

Any hard kill = `LLP_REJECT` or `LLP_CUT`.

### 8. Session Exposure

Default:

* 1–3 LLP bets per day
* 1.5u normal daily cap
* 2.0u hard daily cap only after calibration
* 1.0u max per game
* 1.25u max same-script exposure
* no duplicate same-side exposure

### 9. Re-Approval Rule

`LLP_WATCH` can become `LLP_APPROVED` only through a full rerun.

`LLP_APPROVED` can become `LLP_REJECT` after material change.

If already submitted and later invalidated, log:

`INVALIDATED_AFTER_SUBMISSION`

No chase, hedge, or add-on unless the new position has standalone positive EV.

### 10. Calibration Ledger

Every LLP candidate must log:

* date
* sport
* league
* market
* side
* odds
* line
* book
* opener
* close
* model probability
* no-vig probability
* edge
* stake
* final label
* failure tags
* CLV
* result
* ROI
* Brier/log-loss bucket
* postmortem note

No calibration ledger = no unit scaling.
