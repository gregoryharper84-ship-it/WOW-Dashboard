What belongs in Replit

Replit should enforce the rules mechanically:

canonical event_key generation
duplicate-event detection
opposing-side mutex
Stage 2 probability schema validation
rank_eligible assignment
material-staleness rejection
Postgres calibration ledger
Brier and log-loss calculations
primary-observation deduplication
settlement-worker polling
database table creation
health/status reporting
can_execute=false enforcement

This is the source of truth. Even if ChatGPT gives a bad response, Replit should still reject an invalid run.

What belongs in the Custom GPT

The Custom GPT should know how to call and interpret the backend, and it should mirror the governance in its Instructions.

Add a compact rule block such as:

LLP Stage 2 event-integrity rules are binding.
Do not approve or rank a candidate unless:
- event_key is present and canonical;
- event status is valid;
- slate date is valid in UTC;
- rank_eligible=true;
- all Stage 2 probability fields are valid;
- model_timestamp is not older than latest_material_update_at;
- no opposing side of the same event is selected;
- the calibration and settlement ledgers are available.
RUN_INVALID_OPPOSING_SIDES invalidates the entire run.
STALE_MODEL_OUTPUT caps the row at LLP_REJECT.
Backend results and lower ceilings are controlling.
can_execute=false.
DRY_RUN_ONLY_NO_LIVE_TRADING_NO_MARKET_ORDERS.

The Custom GPT should not try to recreate advisory locks, database writes, settlement daemons, or schema validation in prose.
