Patched. The core change is:

**Kalshi is not “pick the winner.” Kalshi is “only buy the contract when the executable contract price is below our fee-adjusted max buy price.”**

Kalshi’s API orderbook matters because it returns **YES bids and NO bids only**, so the engine must reconstruct asks by reciprocity: `YES ask = 100 - best NO bid`, and `NO ask = 100 - best YES bid`. ([API Documentation][1]) Kalshi’s current fee schedule also makes fees a real edge tax: immediately matched orders are charged trading fees, and the general trading-fee formula is `round up(M × 0.07 × C × P × (1-P))`; the July 2026 schedule lists **KXMLBGAME** and **KXWNBAGAME** with maker/taker multipliers of 1/1. ([Kalshi][2]) Limit orders are the correct default because Kalshi says they let you specify the maximum price you are willing to pay, though they may remain unfilled. ([Kalshi Help Center][3])

The key logic:

```python
best_yes_ask = 100 - best_no_bid
best_no_ask = 100 - best_yes_bid

yes_fee = kalshi_fee(price=yes_ask, contracts=quantity, maker_or_taker="taker")
yes_fee_per_contract = yes_fee / quantity

yes_max_buy = (
    model_probability * 100
    - required_edge_threshold_cents
    - yes_fee_per_contract
    - slippage_buffer_cents
)

if yes_ask <= yes_max_buy and depth >= intended_quantity:
    label = "LLP_PLAYABLE_LIMIT_ONLY"
else:
    label = "LLP_REJECT" or "LLP_WATCH"
```

Add this **before** any more Kalshi sports scans are trusted. Your existing no-vig gate is good, but without this overlay it can still falsely promote a “small edge” that disappears after Kalshi spread and fee. The engine should now classify Kalshi outputs like this:

```text
LLP_REJECT
```

```text
Model says fair price is X¢.
Max dry-run limit price is Y¢.
Current executable ask is Z¢.
```
