## Why it looks like “no results”

There are three likely causes:

### 1. `final_approved_picks` is empty by design

Your engine separates:

* `market_verified`
* `model_qualified`
* `conditional`
* `watch`
* `reject`
* `final_approved_picks`

Your schema supports all of those arrays separately. 

If your frontend/daily task is only reading:

```text
final_approved_picks
```

then it will show **nothing**, even though `model_qualified` has 15 items.

### 2. Model Qualified is not being promoted into the playable card

Under your rules, **Market Verified** requires sportsbook/book, line, price, timestamp, implied probability, model probability, edge, and Kelly. **Model Qualified — PrizePicks** can still be valid when the board/stat path passes but sportsbook price/Kelly is unavailable. 

So if the backend only treats `market_verified` as “final,” it will ignore the 15 Model Qualified props.

### 3. The summary endpoint may be returning counts but not item details

The scan summary gave counts, but the previous response did not list player names/props. That usually means either:

```text
/scan-results/summary
```

is returning counts only, or the GPT prompt summarized counts instead of printing arrays.


Do **not** rename all Model Qualified as Market Verified. Keep the labels honest.

Use this rule:


Or better:
