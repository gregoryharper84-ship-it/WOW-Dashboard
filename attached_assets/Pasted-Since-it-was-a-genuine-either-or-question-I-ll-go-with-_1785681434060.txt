   used, fair/no-vig odds derivation, comparison sportsbook(s), and a
   timestamp. An EV number without all four fields is REJECTED from
   the output, not merely flagged.
4. VARIANCE-VS-SAFETY SEPARATION: A candidate substitution may not be
   described as "safer" or "added safety" unless its raw hit-probability
   lower bound is equal to or higher than the candidate it replaced. A
   swap that lowers hit probability while raising nominal payout must be
   labeled VARIANCE_INCREASE, never a safety upgrade.
5. UPSTREAM DEPENDENCY LOCK: Any output step that depends on a prior
   analysis step must be blocked from displaying "complete" or
   returning results if that prior step is unchecked, timed out, or
   still running. A dependent step returning results under this
   condition must be logged as PIPELINE_INTEGRITY_FAILURE and the
