P1 — Approval quality patch

4. Short-favorite compression gate

Create a specific price bucket for favorites carrying poor payouts.

Suggested requirements:

Breakeven probability	Minimum verified edge	Minimum model probability
52–56%	+1.5%	breakeven + floor
56–60%	+2.0%	breakeven + floor
60–65%	+2.5%	breakeven + floor
65–70%	+3.0%	breakeven + floor
70%+	+3.5%	at least 73.5% when BE is 70%

Hard rule:

Team quality, season record, brand strength, ace status, or public favoritism cannot substitute for verified edge.

Suggested rejection label:

LLP_REJECT_PRICE_COMPRESSION

The Dodgers at 1.43x should fail unless the model probability is at least approximately 73.5% under this table.

7. Market disagreement gate

Require comparison between:

llp_model_prob
consensus_no_vig_prob
prizepicks_breakeven_prob

Calculate:

edge_vs_prizepicks = model_prob - breakeven_prob
edge_vs_market = model_prob - no_vig_prob
platform_price_delta = no_vig_prob - breakeven_prob

Interpretation:

* Model and market both exceed PrizePicks breakeven: strongest case.
* Model exceeds breakeven but market does not: model-disagreement case; downgrade and investigate.
* Market exceeds breakeven but model does not: no LLP approval.
* Neither exceeds breakeven: reject.

Suggested labels:

MARKET_CORROBORATED_EDGE
MODEL_ONLY_DISAGREEMENT
MARKET_ONLY_EDGE
NO_VERIFIED_EDGE

MODEL_ONLY_DISAGREEMENT should cap at LLP_WATCH during the Reliability Freeze.
