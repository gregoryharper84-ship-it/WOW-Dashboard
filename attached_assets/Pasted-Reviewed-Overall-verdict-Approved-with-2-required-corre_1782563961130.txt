## Required correction 1 — CLV formula sign is backwards

Both `wow-llp-ledger` and `wow-postmortem-auto` use:

```text
CLV = entry_odds_implied_prob − closing_odds_implied_prob
```

That reverses the sign for most sportsbook bets.

Example:
You bet a side at **+140**. It closes **+120**.
Entry implied probability = 41.7%.
Closing implied probability = 45.5%.
You **beat the close**, but the current formula gives:

```text
41.7% − 45.5% = -3.8%
```

That would incorrectly mark a good bet as negative CLV.

Correct rule:

```text
CLV = closing_implied_probability − entry_implied_probability
```

Positive CLV = the market closed at a higher implied probability for your side than your entry price.

For spreads/totals/team totals, add line-aware grading:

```text
Spread:
- Bet favorite -2.5, closes -3.5 = Beat Close
- Bet favorite -3.5, closes -2.5 = Lost to Close
- Bet underdog +4.5, closes +3.5 = Beat Close
- Bet underdog +3.5, closes +4.5 = Lost to Close

Total:
- Bet Over 160.5, closes 162.5 = Beat Close
- Bet Over 162.5, closes 160.5 = Lost to Close
- Bet Under 162.5, closes 160.5 = Beat Close
- Bet Under 160.5, closes 162.5 = Lost to Close
```

This is a **must-fix** before relying on calibration.

## Required correction 2 — Opener unavailable should not block CLV grading

`wow-llp-ledger` says if opener is unavailable, CLV cannot be fully graded. That is not quite right. CLV only needs **entry price** and **closing price**.

Correct rule:

```text
Opener unavailable = OPENER_UNAVAILABLE.
This limits opener/market-movement analysis and may cap confidence,
but it does not prevent CLV grading if entry and closing prices are available.

Closing line unavailable = NO_CLOSE_AVAILABLE.
That blocks CLV grading.
```

So:

| Missing field        | Correct impact                      |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Opener missing       | Confidence cap / no opener analysis |
| Closing line missing | CLV cannot be graded                |
| Entry price missing  | CLV cannot be graded                |
| Timestamp missing    | Cannot be approved/playable         |
