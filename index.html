Why LESS Slips Work
🏆 Your $50 win example:
6-pick Flex LESS. All MLB LESS props on May 18. 5/6 hit, Evan Carter DNP = auto-win. Entry $5 → $50.
💰 DNP Insurance
Any player who doesn't start is an automatic LESS win. Targeting fringe/platoon players = built-in insurance.
📉 Blowout Protection
Teams losing big get benched early. Lower-order hitters in blowout losses rarely score or get full plate appearances.
⚾ Game Script Logic
Runs LESS 0.5 on a player hitting 8th for a team facing a Cy Young-caliber starter = high probability LESS.
⚠️ Avoid:
Top-order hitters on strong offenses · Players in high-total games · Anyone with a recent hot hitting streak
