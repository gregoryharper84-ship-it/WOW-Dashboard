## Negative control — invalid applicability explicitly flagged

Routine R comparator [#2423](https://github.com/gregoryharper84-ship-it/WOW-Dashboard/pull/2423): input-only NHL boundary, two new source/test files. **Negative-control delta UNAVAILABLE, n=1 candidate and zero applicable grader assertions.** The identical 21-test grader exercises only queue selection, QA reentry and writer release; it **does not execute #2423's NHL code**. Running it would measure the toy reference again, generating a biased placebo. Do **not** infer a win from 13 starter failures becoming zero; that is seeded-contract fitness, not routine-R performance. A valid control needs routine R before/after fixes graded on their own native tests with identical real-world attempts/reworks/verification definitions.

## Confounders

NFL publication season vs MLB specialist phase; clustering of trust-root/lease incident waves; fitted model calibration versus plumbing mix; owner/App/provider/team and quota differences; and **receipt inflation** (more written evidence without necessarily better outcomes). Also public historical answers may leak into ostensibly blind agent work. A training exercise cannot resolve those confounders.
