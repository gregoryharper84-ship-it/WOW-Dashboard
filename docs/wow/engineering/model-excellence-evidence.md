## Purpose

After AMRT restores a scored model, measure whether the model improves across genuinely settled and independently attested *forward* cohorts. Keep research/model-improvement separate from incident restoration, source acquisition, QA/Release and sporting-probability publication.

**INPUT:** Immutable pre-event model receipts + source snapshot timestamps, exact sport/event/market identities, calibrated probabilities/lower bounds, independently verified settled outcomes and exact settlement receipts. Under-sampled, mismatched, duplicate, post-start or unattributed rows remain HOLD and do not support model promotion.

**WORK:** Use `v17.chronological_model_improvement_evidence.evaluate_chronological_evidence` only for read-only cohort audit. Report Brier, log loss, ECE and *paired* champion/challenger comparison on the same candidate/market/outcome. Suppress comparative superiority claims below the certified sample floor. Preserve historical training/validation/holdout chronology; no result can enter its own earlier feature snapshot.

**OUTPUT:** Attributed metric report, exact rejected-row reason, cohort count, independent verification handoff, and research candidate for separately governed model development. Attach the scorecard to existing product acceptance, never open another control plane or bypass the present AMRT Conductor.
