# WOW V17 — Restoration First (2026-11-18)

## Intent and controlling phase
The product priority is **Phase 1: restore real, reliable V17 sporting-model scoring across sports**, not NFL-only optimization. Reuse existing source, identity, fitted specialist, calibration, Action, immutable receipt, and terminal machinery. There is no new general-purpose prediction model, scheduler, trading permission, or paid data provider.

## Roadmap
1. **RESTORE (P0, now):** NFL, NBA, NHL, NCAAF and NCAAB parallel recovery; other declared sports and existing prop/spread families remain inventoried and typed. Keep NFL in break/fix and acceptance mode, deferring advanced challenger research unless needed for a live correctness failure. Same operational maturity, not identical fitted formulas or equal accuracy.
2. **IMPROVE (after restoration acceptance):** research opponent and game-script input quality, NHL blowout distribution and goalie/prop miss patterns, uncertainty, calibrated bounds and fitted challenger experiments with counterexamples and chronological holdout.
3. **EXPAND (after stable model quality):** additional certified sports/markets, exact-line spreads/props/totals, correlation-aware slips and deeper automation.

## Phase 1 acceptance
A sport × exact supported market is *restored* only after independent proof of: canonical current event/player/line/settlement; verified pregame source and required inputs; the exact fitted specialist and certified artifact; calibrated sporting probability and lower bound; fresh final refresh; immutable pregame model/version receipt; successful real authenticated canonical Action; proper V17_TERMINAL_REDUCER disposition; deployed Render web+worker SHA parity and live Custom GPT Action/editor readback. Market price/value is a distinct downstream status.

Stability means at least three genuine executed scoring/scheduled cycles across two local calendar days where current events exist, not fake fixtures, shadow grades or self-acceptance probes. A complete background job, green CI, 0-row discovery, balanced all-held run, or model artifact registered in source does **not** independently establish restoration. Overall phase closure requires exact supported sport/market reconciliation plus typed blocked/no-current-market states, never a fabricated qualified pick.
